# Pixel People: one small person per agent, posed by what the agent is doing.
#
# Shared by DroidACP and TabDesk (and any app that wants them): a plain text format, so
# both draw the same people from the same data. DroidACP's reader is
# core/.../people/PixelPeople.kt; tools/pixelpeople/preview.py draws every pose as a PNG.
#
# Format: sections start with a keyword line; `grid <label> <x> <y>` is followed by rows
# of one character per pixel, ended by a blank line. "." is transparent. Colours are the
# palette's, except the ones a person's look sets:
#   S s r   skin, its shade and blush (from `option skin`)
#   H h     hair and its shade (from `option haircolor`)
#   T t     shirt and its shade (the chosen `option shirt`, else the agent's colours from
#           `agent`: one colour, or stripes row by row)
#   L       the letter on the shirt (white or dark, whichever shows on the shirt)
#   A a     an accessory's own colours (its `A` and `a` lines)
#
# Every pose is 32 x 32 pixels, drawn in the order of its lines (a grid ends at a blank line):
#   use <name> <x> <y>     a shared part
#   grid base|over <x> <y> pixels of its own
#   @head                  the face, eyes, hair and accessories, at the pose's `head x y`
#                          (a long hair's back is drawn first of all, under everything)
#   letter <x> <y>         the shirt's letter, 3 x 5
#   effect <name> <x> <y>  a bubble or sign
# `eyes open|closed|down` picks the eyes; `head none` draws no person (an empty chair).
#
# The options are what an agent picks once, for its own Pixel Person: skin, hair,
# haircolor, accessories (up to 2), a letter (A-Z, 0-9 or none) and a shirt colour. The
# agent's own colours (`agent <id>`) are drawn as a bar under the person, so people from
# different agents stay apart whatever they wear: 22 x 4 pixels, outlined in `k` (in `G`
# when its colours are as dark as that, so it shows on a dark screen) with its corners left
# out, the colours side by side in the middle two rows (the lower one shaded 0.8). Until an
# agent chooses a shirt, it wears its agent's colours.
